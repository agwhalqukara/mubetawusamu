Career

In the 1960s, she had several appearances on ABC in series like Days of Our Lives, The Odd Couple, and Mulligan’s Stew. She was featured for 12 episodes of the show The Andy Griffith Show on CBS, where she played the role of a pharmacist Ellie Walker.

She signed with NBC in 1963, and the same year she appeared on Redigo followed by another appearance in a medical drama by the name of The Eleventh Hour. She had a role on CBS sitcom series Many Happy Returns in the 1964-1965 season and also guest-appeared on Star Trek in 1967.

In 1990 she appeared in the film Pretty Woman. From 1990-92 she appeared in 35 episodes of the drama series Dr. Quinn, Medicine Woman, where she played as Rebecca Quinn. She lent her voice in the 1992 animated series Eek! the Cat. She published a memoir in 1998 called In The Kitchen with Elinor Donahue. In 2010 she appeared on The Young and the Restless, where she played Judge Anderson.
