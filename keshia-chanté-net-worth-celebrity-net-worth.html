What is Keshia Chanté's Net Worth?

Keshia Chanté is a Canadian musician, actress, and TV personality who has a net worth of $2 million. Keshia Chanté was born in 1988 in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Chanté started performing in front of crowds before her seventh birthday. She began working on her debut album while she was in high school, and released her first single, "Shook (The Answer)", in 2003 when she was 14 years old. Chanté has had close to a dozen top ten singles in her career, including "Unpredictable", "Bad Boy", "Shook", "Been Gone", "Table Dancer", and "Does He Love Me?". She has released three studio albums between 2004 and 2014, including "Keshia Chanté", "2U", and "Night & Day".

Chanté hosts BET's show "106 & Park" with Bow Wow. Her acting credits include appearances in "Cats Cradle", "Da Kink in my Hair", "Soul", and "Sunshine Sketches of a Little Town". She is actively involved with several charities, including World Vision, WarChild, and the Canadian Foundation for AIDS Research. Chanté is the winner of numerous awards, including the Canadian Urban Music Award for Best R&B/Soul Single, the Canadian Urban Music Award for Best New Artist, and the Juno Award for R&B/Soul Recording of the Year. She was invited to the White House holiday party by the Obamas in 2013. Chanté and NHL goalie Ray Emery began dating in 2010.
